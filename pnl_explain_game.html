P&L Explain Trainer – European Gas (NBP / TTF)

Cheat sheet

  • P&L = signed position × price change. Long +, short −. Sense check: long & up = profit.
  • TTF EUR/MWh, 1 lot = 1 MW. MWh = MW × days × 24 (ignore DST here).
  • NBP p/th, 1 lot = 1,000 th/day. Therms = lots × 1,000 × days. GBP P&L = therms × Δp / 100.
  • 1 MWh = 34.1214 therms. p/th = EUR/MWh × EURGBP × 100 / 34.1214.
  • Time spread total = near leg + far leg. Split: spread P&L = near vol × Δ(near−far); residual = (near vol − far vol) × Δfar.
  • New trade = signed vol × (close − trade price).
  • FX P&L (GBP reporting) = opening EUR MTM × (EURGBP today − yesterday); EUR price P&L converted at today’s rate.
  • Roll-down (unchanged curve) = vol × (nearer price − your price) × days elapsed / days between tenors.
  • Daily explain: Actual = New trades + Price moves (per leg) + FX + Carry + Unexplained.
  • Unexplained ÷ a leg’s move = clean lot multiple ⇒ missed trade; leg moved in system not on screen ⇒ mis-mark.
  • Presenting: total → largest drivers → new trades → FX/carry → unexplained + action.
